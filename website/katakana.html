Special Katakana (とくべつなカタカナ — for foreign loanwords)

In the basic chart the w-row has only ワ (wa) and ヲ (wo) — ウィ・ウ・ウェ appear only in these special katakana. yi and ye do not exist.

Long Vowels (長音・ちょうおん)

In katakana, long vowels are written with the bar symbol ー (choonpu).
